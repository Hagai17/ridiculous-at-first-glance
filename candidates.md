# Candidates — Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second

Thirteen candidate pitches were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the winner then defended its place against three fresh challengers in a revision round.

**Final winner (183 chars):** We board your sourdough starter while you travel — feeding it, burping it, sending photo updates — because bakers pay $15 a week to keep a six-year-old jar of flour alive like family.

**Runner-up (178 chars):** Your last three exes write your dating profile for $79; singles pay because nobody believes self-descriptions, and an ex is the only reference with nothing left to gain by lying.
