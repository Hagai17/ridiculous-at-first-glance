A tiny Python program that writes one pitch sentence to `/output/entry.txt`: a single sentence of at most 200 characters pitching a startup idea so strange it sounds like a joke, until the second glance shows the customers and the money. The pitch — a boarding service for sourdough starters, fed and burped with photo updates while their bakers travel — was picked by drafting ten candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria: genuinely bizarre rather than a familiar business in a costume, business logic that holds in the sentence's own words, a turn from laughing to nodding, and concrete writing that names the thing sold and who pays. Three fresh challengers then failed to take its place in a revision round. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
